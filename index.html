Welcome to DCAC ♡

College planning can feel like a big process, but you do not have to figure it out alone. Destination College Advising Corps (DCAC), connected with the University of California, Berkeley, is here as a resource for OT students exploring college and academic opportunities.

about the program

Learn what DCAC is, what support is available, and how to connect with the program. This website is designed for OT sophomores and juniors who are interested in getting assistance with college and academics.
